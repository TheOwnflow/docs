---
title: "What OwnFlow can never do"
description: "The rules your vault enforces."
---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

- The agent cannot withdraw your money, change your rules or your stock list, or send money anywhere except as your rules allow.
- The agent can only spend the stock bucket on purchases, never what is yours to withdraw.
- Every split adds up to exactly 100%.
- Your running costs are yours: the agent can only bill its real cost, within daily limits.
- A purchase that breaks a rule is refused and spends nothing.
- Every movement of money is an event, so everything reconciles.
