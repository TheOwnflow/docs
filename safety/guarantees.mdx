---
title: "What OwnFlow can never do"
description: "The rules your vault enforces by itself."
---

These rules are in the smart contract, not just in the website.

## Your agent can never

- withdraw your money or your stock,
- sell your stock,
- change your split, your stock list or your settings,
- spend anything except the stock bucket on purchases,
- buy a stock you did not approve, or go past your maximum weight or daily limit,
- send your Earn money anywhere except the approved vault,
- bill more than your running costs bucket holds, or more than the daily limit, or the same cost twice.

## OwnFlow can never

- withdraw your money or your stock,
- change your split, your stock list or your settings,
- stop you from withdrawing, selling or leaving.

## What OwnFlow can do, and what you trust it for

- **Stop agent actions** for everyone in an emergency.
- Bill your agents' AI cost from your running costs, within the limits above. The cost figure comes from OwnFlow's records.
- Choose the trading pools and price sources the trading router uses. This decides what a purchase or a sale is checked against.
- Approve which Earn vaults and trading routers may be used, and set the defaults for **new** vaults.
- Set a deposit limit for vaults.

If a trading router is withdrawn, vaults that use it stop buying and selling through it. Your money and stock stay yours and you can withdraw them.

## Everything adds up

- Every split adds up to exactly 100%.
- A purchase that breaks a rule is refused and spends nothing.
- Every movement of money is recorded as an event, so your report can be checked.
