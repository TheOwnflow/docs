These rules are in the smart contracts. They describe the vault code in use today, which can be [updated with notice](#updates-to-the-vault-code).

## Your agent can never

- withdraw or sell,
- change your split, stocks or settings,
- spend anything but the stock part on purchases,
- buy a stock you did not approve, or pass your limits,
- send Earn money anywhere but the approved vault.

## With today's code, OwnFlow can never

- withdraw your money or stock,
- change your split, stocks or settings,
- stop you from withdrawing, selling or leaving.

## What OwnFlow can do

- Stop automatic actions for everyone in an emergency.
- Bill compute units from your running costs, within [limits](/concepts/running-costs). The count comes from OwnFlow's records.
- Choose the pools and price sources every trade is checked against.
- Approve Earn vaults and trading routers, and set defaults for new vaults.
- Set a deposit limit for vaults.
- Release a new version of the vault code, after a waiting period.

If a trading router is withdrawn, vaults that use it stop trading through it. You can still withdraw.

## Pauses

| Action | Your pause | Emergency stop | Safe mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split, buy, lend, bill | Stopped | Stopped | Stopped |
| Withdraw, sell, take everything out | Works | Works | Works |
| Change your rules | Works | Works | Works |
| Create a new vault | Works | Works | Stopped |

- **Your pause:** **Settings** → **Pause my vault**. Choose **Resume my vault** to start again.
- **Emergency stop:** OwnFlow stops automatic actions on every vault.
- **Safe mode:** OwnFlow switches every vault to code that has only your own actions, for a problem in the vault code itself.

Money that arrives during a pause waits in your vault.

## Updates to the vault code

OwnFlow can update the code your vault runs. An update does not move your money.

- A new version is announced on the blockchain first.
- It cannot reach any vault for **at least three days**.
- The app shows the date from which it can take effect. Until then you can take everything out.
- It reaches a few vaults first, then all of them.

The waiting period is your protection. Whoever controls the release key could, after it, release code that does something else with the money in vaults. A guardian role can cancel a proposed version, but **today the same wallet holds both roles**, so it adds no second protection against a stolen key.
