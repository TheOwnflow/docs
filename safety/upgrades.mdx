---
title: "Upgrades and safe mode"
description: "How the code behind your vault can change, and what protects you."
---

Your vault keeps your money and your settings. The code it runs can be updated by OwnFlow, to fix a problem or add a feature. You do not need to do anything for an update, and an update does not move your money.

## Notice before any change

- A new version is announced on the blockchain first.
- It cannot reach any vault before a waiting period: **at least three days** on Base.
- The app shows the date from which it can take effect.
- If you do not want it, you can take everything out before then (**Settings**).
- It then goes to a few vaults first, and to all of them after that.

## Safe mode

In an emergency OwnFlow can switch every vault to safe mode at once.

| | Safe mode |
|---|---|
| Splitting, buying, lending, billing | Stopped |
| Creating a new vault | Stopped |
| Withdraw, sell, take everything out | Works |
| Change your rules | Works |

Money that arrives waits in your vault.

## What you are trusting

The waiting period protects you only if OwnFlow keeps its release keys safe and you can act during it. Whoever controls those keys could, after the waiting period, release code that does something else with the money in vaults. A separate guardian key can cancel a proposed version during the waiting period, but it cannot release one.
