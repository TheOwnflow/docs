You can lose money.

| Risk | What it means |
|---|---|
| Market | A stock token can fall in value |
| Smart contracts | A bug could cause loss. The contracts have **not had an independent audit**. |
| One key | One ordinary wallet holds OwnFlow's [powers](/safety/guarantees). If it is stolen or lost, they go with it. |
| Platform | A mistake in price sources, approved vaults, compute records or new code can cause loss |
| Price source | A price feed can be old or wrong, and a trading pool can be thin |
| Closed markets | Trades can [pause around weekends](/stocks/how-prices-are-checked) while prices elsewhere keep moving |
| Trading cost | Fees and price impact reduce what you get |
| Token issuer | A stock token can be paused or restricted |
| Lending | [Earn](/concepts/earn) yield varies, a position can lose value, and a withdrawal may wait |
| Wallet | If you lose your wallet, you can lose access to your money |
| Network | Outages of Base or of services OwnFlow relies on can pause automatic actions |
| Payments | A payment service can refuse a payment to an agent, for example for sanctions rules |
| Law | Rules about tokenized stocks differ by country and can change |

## Disclosures

- **No investment advice.** OwnFlow recommends no stock, token or strategy. Purchases follow your rules.
- **No guaranteed return.**
- **Availability.** Tokenized stocks are not available to US persons. Following your local laws is your responsibility.
- **No affiliation.** OwnFlow is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the companies whose stock tokens it supports.

## Reduce your risk

Keep your wallet key safe, set limits you are comfortable with, and check your **Activity** and **Report**.
