---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

- **Market risk.** The value of a stock token can fall as well as rise.
- **Smart contract risk.** The contracts have been reviewed by the team but have not yet had an independent audit.
- **Price and liquidity.** A trading pool can be thin, a price feed can be old, and a sale pays the pool's fee and the price impact.
- **Issuer risk.** A stock token can be paused or restricted by its issuer.
- **Yield risk.** Earn yield varies and is not guaranteed.
- **Private-company tokens.** Their reference prices are not live market prices.
