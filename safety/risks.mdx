Putting money into OwnFlow carries risk. You can lose money.

| Risk | What it means |
|---|---|
| Market | A stock token can fall in value as well as rise |
| Smart contract | A bug could cause loss. The contracts have been reviewed by the team but have **not had an independent audit**. |
| Price source | A price feed can be old or wrong, and a trading pool can be thin |
| Trading cost | Fees and price impact reduce what you get, more so in thin markets |
| Token issuer | A stock token can be paused or restricted. It gives economic exposure through a certificate, which is not the same as holding the share with a broker. |
| Lending (Earn) | Yield varies, a position can lose value, and money may not be available to withdraw at once |
| Wallet | If you lose access to the wallet that owns your vault, you can lose access to your money |
| Network | Delays or outages of Base or of services OwnFlow relies on can pause automatic actions |
| Platform | OwnFlow manages the trading router's settings, the approved Earn vaults and the AI cost records. A mistake or a compromised key there can cause loss, within the contract's limits. |
| Rules | Laws about tokenized stocks differ by country and can change |

## Reduce your risk

- Start with a small amount.
- Keep your wallet key safe.
- Set a daily limit and maximum weights you are comfortable with.
- Check your **Activity** and **Report**.
