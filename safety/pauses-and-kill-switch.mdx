---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

- **Your own pause** stops your agent's automatic actions on your vault.
- **The platform kill switch** can stop every agent's actions on every vault at once.

Neither can ever block you. Withdrawing, selling, taking everything out, changing your rules and pausing your own vault always work.
