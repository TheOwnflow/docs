---

## Your own pause

In **Settings**, choose **Pause my vault**. Your agent stops splitting, buying, lending and billing. Choose **Resume my vault** to start again.

While paused:

- new payments wait in your vault and are split after you resume,
- **you** can still withdraw, sell, change your rules and take everything out.

## The emergency stop

OwnFlow can stop every agent's actions on every vault at once, for example if a problem is found. You then see **Platform-wide pause**. Your own actions keep working.

| Action | Your pause | Emergency stop |
|---|---|---|
| Split, buy, lend, bill | Stopped | Stopped |
| Withdraw money or stock | Works | Works |
| Sell, take everything out | Works | Works |
| Change your rules | Works | Works |

## Safe mode

If the problem is in the vault code itself, OwnFlow can switch every vault to [safe mode](/safety/upgrades): nothing happens automatically, no new vault can be made, and all of your own actions keep working.
