---
title: "How prices are checked"
description: "Why a purchase cannot be made at a bad price."
---

A trading pool's price can be pushed around for a moment. OwnFlow therefore checks every purchase and sale against a **reference price** that is not just the pool's price at that moment.

## The checks

<Steps>
  <Step title="A reference price">
    For the stocks OwnFlow starts with, the reference is the stock's Chainlink price feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A floor">
    The trade must return at least the reference amount, less the pool fee and a small allowance. Otherwise it is cancelled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A fresh price">
    If the price feed is too old, the stock is unavailable until it updates. This can happen when the stock market is closed, such as over a weekend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A healthy network">
    No trade happens while the Base network sequencer is down, or for an hour after it comes back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you notice

- A stock can be **Unavailable** for a while. Your money stays in USDC and the purchase happens later.
- A sale can be refused if the market price is below what the check allows. Try again later, or withdraw the tokens.

<Note>
  These checks reduce the risk of a bad price. They do not remove market risk.
</Note>
