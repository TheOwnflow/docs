---
title: "How prices are checked"
description: "Why a purchase cannot be made at a bad price."
---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

Every purchase is checked against a reference price that is not the trading pool's price at that moment.

- The router sets a floor under the reference price. A purchase that cannot meet it is cancelled and your USDC stays.
- The reference is the stock's Chainlink price feed. An old feed (for example over a weekend) makes the stock unavailable until it updates.
- Nothing is bought while the Base sequencer is down or has just come back.
