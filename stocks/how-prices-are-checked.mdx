---
title: "Prices and trading hours"
description: "How trades are protected, and when they pause."
---

A trading pool's price can be pushed around for a moment. OwnFlow therefore checks every purchase and sale against an outside **reference price**.

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Reference price | The stock's Chainlink price feed |
| Floor | A trade must return at least the reference amount, less the pool fee and **2%** |
| Fresh price | A price older than **26 hours** is not used |
| Healthy network | No trade while the Base sequencer is down, or for an hour after it returns |

A trade that fails a check does not happen. Your money or tokens stay in your vault.

## Weekends and holidays

Stock tokens trade on Base at any hour, but the price feeds follow the stock market and stop updating while it is closed.

- At the start of a weekend, trades continue for a while on the last price.
- Once that price is older than 26 hours, **buying and selling through OwnFlow pause**. On recent weekends this lasted about a day.
- Holidays can pause trading too.
- Trading starts again by itself when the feed updates.

During a pause you can still withdraw stock tokens and USDC, and use Earn. Your agents keep earning.

<Note>
  These checks reduce the risk of a bad price. They do not remove market risk.
</Note>
