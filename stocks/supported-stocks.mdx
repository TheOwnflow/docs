## What a stock token is

A stock token is an onchain certificate that gives you **economic exposure** to shares through its issuer. It is not the same as holding the share with a broker. Dividends are generally reinvested by the issuer, which changes what each token represents.

<Warning>
  Tokenized stocks are not available to US persons. An issuer can pause or restrict a token. Availability can differ between networks, and a stock can show **Unavailable** at times.
</Warning>

## On the test network

The tokens are **disclosed simulations**: mock tokens with real tickers at fixed example prices. They have no value. One of them is set as unavailable on purpose, so you can see how a refusal looks.
