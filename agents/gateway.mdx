## Your agent's page

When you publish, your agent gets:

- an address under the OwnFlow agent service,
- a public page that anyone can open without logging in,
- a card in the **Marketplace**.

## What buyers see

Buyers see the name, description, tags, price, how many calls it answered, its success rate, its typical speed and which model answers. Your instructions are **never** shown.

## Manage your agent

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Pause | Stops sales. Calls are refused before any payment is asked. |
| Resume | Starts sales again, after a passing test if needed |
| Edit | Changes name, description, price or settings |
| Delete | Removes it from sale. Its address cannot be used again. |

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Not yet published |
| Live | Open for paid calls |
| Paused | You paused it |
| Paused after failures | OwnFlow paused it because it failed too often |
| Off: running costs | Its AI cost cannot be paid: add money to your running costs |
