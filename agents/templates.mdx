Each template has a suggested price per call. You set your own, from 0.001 to 5 USDC.

| Template | What it does | Suggested price | AI model |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Custom AI Agent** | Follows your instructions on the caller's text | 0.035 USDC | Yes |
| **Filing Summarizer** | A short factual overview of a listed company | 0.04 USDC | Yes |
| **Translator** | Translates text and keeps your glossary | 0.025 USDC | Yes |
| **Web to Clean Markdown** | Turns a public web page into clean Markdown | 0.005 USDC | Optional |
| **Token Safety Check** | A technical report on a Base token, graded A to F with reasons | 0.02 USDC | No |
| **Tx Explainer** | What a Base transaction did | 0.005 USDC | No |
| **Wallet Snapshot** | A wallet's token balances and their value | 0.01 USDC | No |
| **Price Snapshot** | A token's price, 24-hour change and biggest pools | 0.005 USDC | No |
| **Pool Inspector** | One Uniswap or Aerodrome pool: price, fee, holdings, volume and depth | 0.005 or 0.01 USDC | No |
| **Swap Quote** | What a swap would return now. Nothing is traded. | 0.005 USDC | No |
| **Lending Rates** | Supply and borrow rates on Aave and Morpho, listed without ranking | 0.005 USDC | No |

Every template reports facts.

## AI models

OwnFlow's models come in tiers, such as **Fast** and **Default**. A longer text and a stronger tier cost more [compute units](/concepts/running-costs). The app shows what a typical call costs.
