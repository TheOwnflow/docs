The **Earn** part of your split is lent into a USDC lending vault on [Morpho](https://morpho.org) that has been approved for OwnFlow.

<Warning>
  Yield varies and is **not guaranteed**. A position can be worth less than you put in.
</Warning>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set aside">
    The Earn share stays in your vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lent">
    From 1.00 USDC, your agent lends it. It can only put money **into** the approved vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw">
    On **Funds**, withdraw part or all of your position to your wallet, even while your vault is paused.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The approved vault today is **Gauntlet USDC Prime**: [`0x050cE30b927Da55177A4914EC73480238BAD56f0`](https://basescan.org/address/0x050cE30b927Da55177A4914EC73480238BAD56f0). The Earn card links to its page on Morpho, with its yield, curator, fee and collateral.

## Good to know

- If the lending vault is full or unavailable, the money stays idle. You can move it to what you can withdraw.
- A lending vault may not have enough free money for a withdrawal at a given moment. Try again later.
- The position belongs to your vault's address, so the Morpho app does not show it under your wallet.
- Extra reward tokens from a Morpho program would be assigned to your vault. Today's vault code cannot claim them.
- Before your first use, you accept a notice: OwnFlow's terms and [Morpho's Disclaimer](https://morpho.org/disclaimers/) apply. OwnFlow is developed and operated separately from Morpho.

## Risks

| Risk | What it means |
|---|---|
| Smart contracts | A bug in Morpho's or OwnFlow's contracts can lose money |
| Market | Borrowers' collateral can fall fast, and the loss can reach lenders |
| Curator | The curator decides where the money is lent and can change it |
| Liquidity | A withdrawal may have to wait |
| Price feeds | A wrong oracle price can cause a loss |
