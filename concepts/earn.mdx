title: "Earn"
description: "A part of each payment that is lent into an approved vault."
---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

The Earn share is set aside and your agent can lend it into a vault that the platform approved (a Morpho USDC vault on Base). The agent can only put money in. Only you can take it out.

<Warning>
  Yield varies and is not guaranteed. OwnFlow never shows a fixed return.
</Warning>

If the Earn vault is unavailable, the money stays idle and you can move it to what is yours to withdraw at any time.
