title: "Earn"
description: "A share of each payment that is lent into an approved vault."
---

The **Earn** part of your split is set aside, then lent by your agent into a USDC lending vault (a Morpho vault on Base) that has been approved for OwnFlow.

<Warning>
  Yield varies and is **not guaranteed**. A position can also be worth less than you put in. OwnFlow never shows a fixed return.
</Warning>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set aside">
    When a payment is split, the Earn share is kept in your vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lent">
    Your agent lends it once at least 1.00 USDC is set aside. It can only put money **into** the approved vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You take it out">
    On **Funds**, the Earn card lets you withdraw part or all of your position to your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- If the Earn vault is unavailable or full, the money stays idle in your vault. You can move it to what you can withdraw.
- You can withdraw from Earn at any time, even while your vault is paused. A lending vault may not have enough free money at a given moment. In that case try again later.
- The card shows what is lent now, what is set aside, and what you earned so far. That number can be negative.
- On the test network the Earn vault is a **disclosed simulation**.
