description: "How every payment is divided."
---

Every payment your vault receives is divided into five parts that add up to **exactly 100%**.

| Part | What it is for | Starting value | Can you change it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform fee | OwnFlow's [fee](/concepts/fees) | 1% | No |
| Running costs | Your agents' [compute units](/concepts/running-costs) | 20% | Yes |
| Stock | [Rule-based purchases](/concepts/stock-baskets) | 50% | Yes |
| Earn | [Lending](/concepts/earn) | 20% | Yes |
| Yours to withdraw | Your own USDC | 9% | Yes |

If no Earn vault is available, Earn starts at 0% and that share stays with what you can withdraw. Rounding remainders go there too.

## Change your split

Open **Rules**, move the sliders to a total of 100%, click **Save allocation** and sign. The new split applies to payments split after that. The page warns you when running costs are below 20%.

## When things happen

Your agent waits until an amount is worth the transaction.

| Step | Happens from |
|---|---|
| Splitting a payment | 1.00 USDC waiting |
| Buying a stock | 0.50 USDC for each stock |
| Lending to Earn | 1.00 USDC set aside |
| Billing running costs | 0.25 USDC of compute units |

A split usually takes a few minutes. Anyone can also click **Split it now** on your pay page. Nothing is split while your vault is paused.
