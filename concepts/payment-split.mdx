---
title: "The payment split"
description: "How every payment is divided, and what each part is for."
---

Every payment your vault receives is divided into five parts. They always add up to **exactly 100%**.

| Part | What it is for | Starting value | Can you change it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform fee | OwnFlow's fee | 1% | No, it is fixed when your vault is created |
| Running costs | Your agents' [compute units](/concepts/running-costs) | 20% | Yes |
| Stock | Rule-based purchases | 50% | Yes |
| Earn | Lent into an approved vault | 20% | Yes |
| Yours to withdraw | Your own USDC | The rest (9%) | Yes |

If no Earn vault is available on a network, Earn starts at 0% and that share stays with what you can withdraw.

## Change your split

Open **Rules**. Move the sliders or use the minus and plus buttons. The summary at the top follows you as you scroll and shows:

- **Adds up to 100%** when you can save,
- **N% left** when some is not assigned yet,
- **N% over** when you assigned too much.

Click **Save allocation** and sign. The new split applies to payments split **after** that. Money already divided stays where it is.

## When is a payment split?

- Automatically, usually within a few minutes, once at least **1.00 USDC** is waiting.
- At once, if anyone clicks **Split it now** on your pay page.
- Not while your vault is paused. The money waits and is split after you resume.

## Smallest amounts

Your agent waits until an amount is worth the transaction.

| Step | Happens from |
|---|---|
| Splitting a payment | 1.00 USDC waiting |
| Buying a stock | 0.50 USDC |
| Lending to Earn | 1.00 USDC set aside |
| Billing running costs | 1,000,000 CU (1.00 USDC) built up, or 100,000 CU when the bucket has run out |

While money waits to be split, **Overview** says how much is waiting and why.

## Rounding

Amounts are worked out to the smallest unit of USDC. Any rounding remainder goes to **yours to withdraw**.

<Warning>
  The fee applies to **every** USDC your vault splits, including money you add yourself.
</Warning>
