Every payment that reaches your vault is divided into five parts that always add up to exactly 100%.

| Part | What it is | Starting value |
|---|---|---|
| Platform fee | Fixed when the vault is created. You cannot change it for that vault. | 1% |
| Running costs | Your own money, kept to pay your agents' real compute cost. | 5% |
| Stock | Used for rule-based purchases from your list. | 50% |
| Earn | Set aside to be lent into an approved vault. | 20% |
| Yours to withdraw | USDC you can take at any time. | The rest |

You can change the four parts you control at any time. The app shows how much is left to assign, or by how much you are over, while you move the sliders.
