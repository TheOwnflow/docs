Your vault is a smart contract on Base that only you own. It receives USDC, splits it by your rules and holds the stock tokens that are bought.

- **Only you** can withdraw, change the rules, change the stock list, change the agent and pause the vault.
- **Your agent** can only split payments, buy stock inside your rules, lend the Earn share into an approved vault and bill its real running costs.
- Nothing the platform does can lock you in: your own actions never depend on the platform being online.
