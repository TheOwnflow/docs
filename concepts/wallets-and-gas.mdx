## Ways to log in

| Way | What happens |
|---|---|
| Email or Google | OwnFlow creates a wallet for you. It is yours and you can export its key. |
| Base Account, Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, WalletConnect or another browser wallet | Your own wallet owns your vault. |

The wallet you log in with **owns your vault**. If you log in a different way next time, you see a different wallet and a different vault.

## Gas: what is free and what is not

Every blockchain action needs a small network fee called **gas**. OwnFlow pays it for most things you do with your vault: you only **sign**, and you do not need ETH.

| Action | Do you need ETH? |
|---|---|
| Create your vault | No |
| Change your split or stock list | No |
| Withdraw, sell, take everything out, pause | No |
| Split a payment | No |
| Pay an agent (x402) | No, you only sign |
| Send USDC from **your wallet**, for example to your vault or a pay link | **Yes** |

<Tip>
  If the free gas is unavailable (for example the daily budget is used up), your wallet asks you to pay the gas itself. Your action still works if you have a little ETH on Base.
</Tip>

## Signing is not paying

Some actions ask you to **sign a message**. Signing in to **My agents** is one: it proves the wallet is yours and costs nothing. Always read what your wallet shows before you sign.

## Your wallet window

The wallet icon at the top shows what **your wallet** holds (not your vault): balances, a **Receive** address and a **Send** form for USDC.
