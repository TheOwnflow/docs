title: "Stock baskets and rules"
description: "Which stocks your vault may buy, and the limits it must keep."
---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

You choose the stocks. The agent never picks one.

- **A list of stocks** your vault may buy (up to 20).
- **A target weight** and a **maximum weight** for each stock.
- **A daily limit** on how much may be spent in one day.

A basket takes effect only when you sign it. A purchase that would break a limit is refused and spends nothing. If a market is closed, a stock is paused or a swap fails, the money stays in USDC and the vault does not retry in a loop.
