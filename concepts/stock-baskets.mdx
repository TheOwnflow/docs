---
title: "Stock rules"
description: "Which stocks your agent may buy, and the limits it keeps."
---

You choose the stocks. Your agent never picks one.

## Your basket

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stocks | The only stocks your agent may buy (up to 20) |
| Target weight | The share you want each stock to reach. Targets add up to 100%. |
| Maximum weight | The most a stock may be of your stock side |
| Daily limit | The most that can be spent on stocks in one UTC day. At 0, nothing is bought. |

Your **stock side** is the value of the stocks you hold plus the USDC waiting to buy more. A purchase that would push a stock above its maximum weight is refused.

## Change your basket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose">
    In **Rules**, tick your stocks and set your daily limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the shares">
    Click **Next: set the shares**. **Share %** is how much of your stock money goes to a stock. **Limit %** is the share it can never be bought past.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    Click **Approve this basket**. It takes effect only after you sign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new basket does not sell anything. A removed stock stays in your vault until you [sell or withdraw](/guides/sell-and-withdraw) it.

## How your agent buys

- It buys the stocks furthest below their targets first.
- It spends only the stock part of your vault.
- Each purchase is at least 0.50 USDC.
- A stock that is unavailable keeps its share in USDC for later.

## Why a purchase is refused

| Reason in Activity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not in your basket | You did not approve this stock |
| Daily limit | Today's limit is used up |
| Maximum weight | The stock would go over its maximum |
| Not enough in the stock bucket | There is not enough USDC |
| Token paused | The issuer paused the token |
| Unavailable | The stock cannot be traded safely now |
| Price moved | The price moved more than allowed |
| Swap failed | The trade did not go through |

A refused purchase spends nothing. Your agent waits an hour before it tries that stock again.
