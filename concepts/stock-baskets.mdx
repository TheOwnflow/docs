---
title: "Stock baskets and rules"
description: "Which stocks your agent may buy, and the limits it must keep."
---

You choose the stocks. Your agent never picks one.

## What a basket contains

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stocks | The only stocks your agent may buy (up to 20) |
| Target weight | The share you want each stock to reach. Targets add up to 100%. |
| Maximum weight | The most a stock may be of your stock side |
| Daily limit | The most that can be spent on stocks in one day |

A basket takes effect **only when you sign it**.

## What "weight" means

Your **stock side** is the value of the stocks you hold plus the USDC still waiting in the stock bucket. A stock's weight is its value as a share of that total. A purchase that would push a stock above its maximum weight is refused.

**Example.** Your stock side is 100.00 USDC and Apple's maximum is 60%. A purchase that would make Apple 67% of the stock side is refused and nothing is spent.

## How your agent buys

- It looks at which stocks are furthest below their targets and buys those first.
- It spends only the stock bucket, never your withdrawals, running costs or Earn.
- It buys amounts of at least 0.50 USDC.
- It stays under your daily limit. A day is counted in UTC.
- If a stock is unavailable, its share stays in USDC for later.

## Why a purchase is refused

| Reason in Activity | What it means |
|---|---|
| Not in your basket | You did not approve this stock |
| Daily limit | Today's limit is used up |
| Maximum weight | The stock would go over its maximum |
| Not enough in the stock bucket | The bucket cannot pay for it |
| Token paused | The issuer paused the token |
| Unavailable | The stock cannot be traded safely right now |
| Price moved | The price moved more than allowed |
| Swap failed | The trade did not go through |

A refused purchase spends nothing. Your agent waits an hour before it tries that stock again.

<Warning>
  With a daily limit of **0**, nothing can be bought.
</Warning>
