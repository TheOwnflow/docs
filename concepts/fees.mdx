---
title: "Fees"
description: "What OwnFlow charges, and what it does not."
---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

- **Platform fee:** a percentage of each payment your vault receives (1% to start). It is fixed when your vault is created.
- **Selling stock is not income.** No platform fee is taken on a sale.
- **Network costs:** for a wallet that is a plain key, OwnFlow's relayer pays the gas of your signed actions. If the relayer is unavailable, your wallet pays its own gas.
- **Swaps:** buying or selling pays the trading pool's fee and the price impact. These go to the pool, not to OwnFlow.
