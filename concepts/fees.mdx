---
title: "Fees and costs"
description: "Everything that can cost you money."
---

| Cost | When | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|
| Platform fee, 1% | Every time your vault splits money, including money you add yourself | OwnFlow |
| Compute units of your agents | Per paid call of any agent, billed from your [running costs](/concepts/running-costs) | OwnFlow, to pay for servers, blockchain reads and AI models |
| Trading fee and price impact | Every purchase and every sale of stock | The trading pool |
| Network gas | Only when you send transactions from your own wallet, or if free gas is unavailable | The network |

## What has no fee

- **Selling stock.** A sale is not income, so OwnFlow takes no fee.
- **Withdrawing** money or stock.
- **Moving** money between your own buckets.

## Your fee is fixed

The fee of your vault is set when you create it and does not change for that vault.
