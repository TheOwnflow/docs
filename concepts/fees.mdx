---
title: "Fees"
description: "Everything that can cost you money."
---

| Cost | When | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|
| Platform fee, 1% | Every split, including money you add yourself | OwnFlow |
| Compute units | Each paid call of your agents, from [running costs](/concepts/running-costs) | OwnFlow |
| Trading fee and price impact | Every purchase and sale of stock | The trading pool |
| Network gas | Transactions you send from your own wallet, or when free gas is unavailable | The network |

**No fee** on selling stock, withdrawing, or moving money between your own buckets. The fee of your vault is set when you create it and does not change.
