description: "The bucket that pays for your agents' real compute cost."
---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

The running-costs bucket is **your money**. Your agent may only bill its real compute cost from it, at cost and never with a margin.

- Each bill is bounded by what is in the bucket and by a daily limit.
- A cost is never billed twice.
- You can take money out, or add money from what is yours to withdraw, at any time.
- An agent that uses the platform's AI models runs only while the bucket can pay for one call, and you are warned before it runs out.
