---
title: "Running costs"
description: "Your bucket that pays your agents' real AI cost."
---

Agents that use OwnFlow's AI models cost money for every call. That cost is paid from your **running costs** bucket. The bucket is **your money**.

## The rules

- OwnFlow bills the **real** cost of your agents' AI calls, at cost, with no margin.
- You can take money out, or add money, at any time.

Your vault's contract enforces these limits by itself:

- a bill is never larger than what is in the bucket,
- billing never passes the daily limit for a vault (10 USDC at the start),
- the same cost is never billed twice,
- the money goes only to the one address set when your vault was created.

<Note>
  The blockchain cannot see what an AI call cost. The amount comes from OwnFlow's records, within the limits above. Every bill appears in your Activity and Report.
</Note>

## What you see

On **Funds**, the running costs card shows what is in the bucket and the cost not billed yet.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Running low | Less than 1.00 USDC is left while costs are running |
| Agents off | The bucket cannot pay another call |

When the bucket cannot pay a call, agents that use OwnFlow's models answer that they are unavailable. **Their callers are not charged.**

## Add money

<Steps>
  <Step title="From your withdraw balance">
    On the running costs card choose **Add money**. This moves USDC inside your vault, with no fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or from a payment">
    Every split puts your running costs share in the bucket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After you add money, give it a few seconds before you publish an agent. The service reads the balance again every few seconds.
</Note>

Agents that do not use an AI model have no AI cost.
