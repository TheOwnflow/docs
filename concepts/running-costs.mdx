---
title: "Running costs"
description: "Your bucket that pays for running your agents."
---

Every paid call of your agents costs **compute units (CU)**, paid from your **running costs**. That money is yours until it is billed.

**1,000,000 CU = 1.00 USDC.**

## What a call costs

| What the call does | CU |
|---|---|
| Runs | 200 |
| Each started second | 10 |
| Each read of the blockchain | 20 (45 for a search of past events) |
| Each web request | 20 |
| Uses an OwnFlow AI model | The model's price plus 10% |
| Is answered from the cache | 50 in total |
| Fails, or is your own test | Nothing |

Your agent's page shows what its paid calls cost on average. The rates can change; this page and the app's Terms show the current ones.

## Billing

OwnFlow bills your vault each time **250,000 CU** (0.25 USDC) build up. When your running costs run out, it collects what is left from 100,000 CU.

- A bill your running costs cannot pay stays owed, and the app shows **Bill due**.
- At **500,000 CU** (0.50 USDC) unpaid, the app shows **Agents paused**. Callers are not charged.
- Your agents start again once the bill is paid.

This credit lets a new vault start before its first income.

## Add money

On the running costs card, click **Add money**:

- **From your wallet:** you sign, and the USDC goes straight into running costs. No fee.
- **From your withdraw balance:** moves USDC inside your vault. No fee.

Each split also adds your running costs share. Compare that share with what a call costs: at a price of 0.005 USDC, 20% is 1,000 CU per call.

## Limits

Your vault never pays a bill larger than the bucket or the same cost twice, and bills go only to the address set when the vault was created. The blockchain cannot see what a call used, so the count comes from OwnFlow's records. Every bill appears in **Activity** and **Report**.
