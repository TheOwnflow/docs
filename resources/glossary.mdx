---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The words used in OwnFlow."
---

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Agent | A service made from a template that people pay to use |
| Basket | The stocks your agent may buy, with target weights, maximum weights and a daily limit |
| Base | The blockchain network OwnFlow runs on |
| Compute unit (CU) | The measure of what a call uses; 1,000,000 CU is 1.00 USDC |
| Daily limit | The most that can be spent on stocks in one UTC day |
| Disclosed simulation | A clearly marked mock, used on test networks |
| Earn | The share of payments lent into an approved vault |
| Gas | The network fee of a blockchain action |
| Maximum weight | The largest share a stock may have of your stock side |
| Price feed | An outside source of a stock's price, used to check trades |
| Running costs | Your bucket that pays for running your agents |
| Split | How each payment is divided into five parts |
| Stock side | Your stocks plus the USDC waiting to buy more |
| USDC | A digital dollar |
| Vault | Your own smart contract that holds your USDC and stock |
| Worker | The automatic helper that splits, buys, lends and bills within your rules |
| x402 | A standard for paying for a web request with USDC |
