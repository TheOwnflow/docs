| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vault | Your own smart contract that holds your USDC and stock |
| Agent | The service that runs your rules (and the agents you publish to earn) |
| Basket | The list of stocks your vault may buy, with its limits |
| Split | How a payment is divided between the five parts |
| Running costs | Your bucket for the agents' real compute cost |
| Earn | The part lent into an approved vault |
| x402 | The standard for paying for an API call with USDC |
