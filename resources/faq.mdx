<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which USDC do I use?">
    USDC issued by Circle on **Base**, at the address on [Contracts](/resources/networks-and-contracts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My payment arrived but was not split.">
    A split needs 1.00 USDC waiting and usually takes a few minutes. It does not happen during a pause. You can click **Split it now** on your pay page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was nothing bought?">
    Check **Activity**. Common reasons: less than 0.50 USDC for a stock (two equal stocks need 1.00 USDC), the daily limit, a maximum weight, an unavailable stock or a paused vault.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not buy or sell on a weekend?">
    The reference price is too old. See [Prices and trading hours](/stocks/how-prices-are-checked). You can still withdraw the tokens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my Earn deposit not in the Morpho app?">
    Your vault made the deposit, not your wallet. See it on the Earn card, or look up your vault's address on Basescan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not publish my agent?">
    You need a passing test from the last 30 minutes, and running costs that can pay for a call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A call says the payment is pending.">
    It was sent but not confirmed in time. Check the transaction it names before you pay again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My vault is gone.">
    A vault belongs to one wallet. Log in the same way you did when you created it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My wallet asks me to pay gas.">
    Free gas may be unavailable for a moment. A transfer from your own wallet always needs gas.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Messages in the app

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Your vault is paused | You paused it. Withdrawals still work. |
| Platform-wide pause | Automatic actions are off for everyone. You can still withdraw. |
| Safe mode | Only your own actions work |
| A new version of the vault code | An [update](/safety/guarantees#updates-to-the-vault-code) was announced |
| Bill due | Your running costs cannot pay a bill |
| Agents paused | 0.50 USDC of running costs is unpaid |
| Catching up | The records are a little behind the blockchain. Wait a moment. |
