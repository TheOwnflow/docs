---
title: "Sell and withdraw"
description: "Get your money and stock out, in part or in full."
---

Only you can do these.

| Action | Where | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Withdraw USDC | **Funds** → **Withdraw to my wallet** | What is in **yours to withdraw** goes to your wallet |
| Withdraw stock | **Overview** → **Withdraw** | The stock tokens go to your wallet |
| Sell stock | **Overview** → **Sell** | The USDC goes to **yours to withdraw** |

## Sell stock

Choose how much with the slider. Before you confirm, you see how many tokens you sell, about what they are worth, the **least** you accept and what stays.

- A sale pays the market price less the trading fee and price impact.
- A sale that would pay less than your minimum is refused.
- Your agent cannot spend the money from a sale.
- Sales can pause [around weekends](/stocks/how-prices-are-checked). Withdrawing the tokens still works.

## Take everything out

In **Settings**, choose **Take everything out**. One signature does this in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pause your vault">
    Your agent stops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect idle money">
    Unspent stock and Earn money move to what you can withdraw.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell your stocks (optional)">
    The stocks in your basket are sold for USDC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave Earn">
    As much of your position as the lending vault can pay now. The rest stays yours, to withdraw later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to you">
    Your USDC and running costs, and the remaining stocks if you choose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A part that cannot be done is skipped. A stock that is no longer in your basket stays until you withdraw or sell it. You can resume your vault later.
