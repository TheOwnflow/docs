title: "Sell, withdraw and exit"
description: "Take USDC or stock out, in full or in part."
---

<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

- **Withdraw USDC** from what is yours to withdraw, at any time.
- **Withdraw stock** to your wallet. Use the slider to send any share from 0% to 100%.
- **Sell stock** for USDC. Use the slider to sell any share. The USDC goes to what is yours to withdraw and is not income, so no platform fee is taken.
- **Take everything out** pauses your vault and sends your money to your wallet in one signature. You can choose to sell your stocks first.

A sale is your own decision, never a recommendation.
