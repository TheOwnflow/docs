---
title: "Sell, withdraw and take everything out"
description: "Get your money and stock out, in part or in full."
---

You can always take your money and stock out. You do not need ETH for these actions.

## Withdraw USDC

On **Funds**, choose **Withdraw to my wallet**. You can withdraw what is in **yours to withdraw**.

## Withdraw stock

On **Overview**, find the stock and click **Withdraw**. Use the slider or the **25%**, **50%**, **75%** and **All** buttons. The stock tokens move to your wallet.

## Sell stock for USDC

On **Overview**, click **Sell** and choose how much with the slider. Before you confirm, you see:

- how many tokens you sell,
- about what they are worth,
- the **least** you accept: the sale is refused if it would pay less,
- how much stays in your vault.

The USDC goes to **yours to withdraw**. Your agent cannot spend it. A sale is your own decision, never a recommendation, and has no OwnFlow fee.

<Warning>
  A sale pays the market price less the trading fee and the price impact. For a stock with a thin market this can be noticeable. When a stock cannot be priced safely, for example while its price feed is old, it cannot be sold through OwnFlow at that moment. You can still withdraw the tokens.
</Warning>

## Take everything out

In **Settings**, choose **Take everything out**. One signature does all of this, in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pause your vault">
    Your agent stops, so nothing changes while you leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect idle money">
    Unspent stock money and unlent Earn money move to what you can withdraw.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell your stocks (optional)">
    If you choose, the stocks in your basket are sold for USDC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave Earn">
    Your Earn position is withdrawn as far as the lending vault can pay right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to you">
    Your withdraw balance and running costs go to your wallet. If you choose, the remaining stocks do too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If one part cannot be done (for example one stock cannot be sold), it is skipped and the rest goes on. You can **resume** your vault later with the same rules.

<Note>
  Take everything out covers the stocks in your current basket. A stock you hold that is no longer in your basket stays until you withdraw or sell it.
</Note>
