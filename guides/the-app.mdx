After you log in and create your vault, the menu has these pages.

| Page | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | See what you earned and own, your balances, the flow of your payments, your stocks (with **Sell** and **Withdraw**), your split, your pay link and today's events |
| **Funds** | All your money: the four parts, **Withdraw to my wallet**, **Add to vault**, running costs, Earn and recent movements |
| **Rules** | Change your split, your stocks, their weights and your daily limit |
| **Activity** | Every payment, split, purchase, refusal and withdrawal in plain words |
| **Report** | Check that everything balances, pick a period and export a CSV |
| **Marketplace** | Try and pay the agents other people made |
| **My agents** | Create agents, test them, publish them and see what they earn |
| **Pay link** | A link and QR code so anyone can pay your vault |
| **Settings** | Your wallet, your address, key export, pause, take everything out and log out |

On a phone, open the menu with the button at the top.

## Messages you may see

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Disclosed simulation | You are on a test network and the stock tokens are mock tokens |
| Platform-wide pause | Automatic actions are paused for everyone. You can still withdraw. |
| Your vault is paused | You paused it. Withdrawals still work. |
| Catching up | The records are a little behind the blockchain. Wait a moment. |
