title: "Set your rules"
description: "Change your split and your stock list."
---

Open **Rules**.

## Change your split

Move the sliders until the total is 100%, then click **Save allocation** and sign. **Reset** goes back to your saved split.

## Change your stocks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your preferences">
    Pick the stocks, the largest number of stocks, the largest share one stock may have and your daily limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft the basket">
    Click **Draft basket**. The app proposes equal target weights for the stocks you picked. It does not rank or recommend stocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust it">
    Change each target and maximum if you want. Targets must add up to 100%, and a maximum cannot be below its target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and sign">
    The change takes effect only after you sign it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a change does not do

A new basket does not sell stocks you already hold. A stock you removed stays in your vault until you [sell or withdraw](/guides/sell-and-withdraw) it.
