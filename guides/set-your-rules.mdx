Open **Rules**.

## Change your split

Move the sliders until the total is 100%, then click **Save allocation** and sign. **Reset** goes back to your saved split. The page warns you when running costs are below 20%.

## Change your stocks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the stocks">
    Tick the stocks your agent may buy. Set your **daily spending limit**. With two or more stocks you can also set a safety limit: the most any one stock may take.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the shares">
    Click **Next: set the shares**. Every stock starts with an equal share. **Share %** is how much of your stock money goes to a stock. **Limit %** is the share it can never be bought past. Shares add up to 100%. **Make them equal** resets them. The app does not rank or recommend stocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and sign">
    Click **Approve this basket**. The change takes effect only after you sign it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a change does not do

A new basket does not sell stocks you already hold. A stock you removed stays in your vault until you [sell or withdraw](/guides/sell-and-withdraw) it.
