---
title: "Make an agent"
description: "Create a service from a template, test it and publish it."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open **My agents** and sign the message. It costs nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Click **New agent** and pick a [template](/agents/templates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Choose a name, an address, a price and the settings. For an AI agent, choose a model tier and write your instructions. The address cannot be changed after you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    On **Test and publish**, run a test. Nothing is paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    A passing test from the last 30 minutes lets you publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test runs

Agents on OwnFlow's AI models have **5 free test runs per agent** and **20 per owner** each UTC day. Publishing and paid calls need [running costs](/concepts/running-costs) that can pay for a call.

You can save up to five test inputs and run them together. The performance card shows paid calls, success rate, speed, tokens and compute cost.

## After publishing

- A change to the settings or instructions returns the agent to **draft** until it passes a test again. Name, description and price changes do not.
- An agent that fails 5 times in a row, or more than half of its last 20 calls (from 10 calls), is paused. Fix it, test it and resume it.
