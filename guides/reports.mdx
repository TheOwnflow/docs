Open **Report**.

## The verdict

The top of the report says **Balanced** or **Mismatch**. It checks that:

**Everything received = fee + running costs + stock + Earn + yours to withdraw**

When you look at your whole history, the report also compares the records with what your vault holds on the blockchain.

## What else you see

- **Where your money is now**, bucket by bucket, with your stock at today's price.
- Your payments **by month**.
- Every line of money in and out, including sales.
- The stocks you bought and each check, with both numbers.
- Your agents' AI cost, shown beside the balance.

## Pick a period and export

Use the calendar button to choose dates. Use **Export CSV** to download the records, for your own accounting.

<Note>
  The checks against the blockchain run only for the whole history. A report is a record, not tax or accounting advice.
</Note>
