title: "Get paid"
description: "Two ways to receive USDC into your vault."
---

## Way 1: publish an agent

Share your agent's page. Each successful call is paid in USDC with [x402](/agents/pricing-and-payments) and goes to your vault. If the agent fails, the caller is **not charged**. Other AI agents can call yours too: the page has ready instructions to copy.

## Way 2: share your pay link

Open **Pay link**. You get a link and a QR code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Anyone can open the link. They do not need an OwnFlow vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They pay">
    They send USDC from their wallet to your vault. Their wallet needs a little ETH for gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It is split">
    The payment is split by your rules, usually within a few minutes, or at once with **Split it now**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The pay page first checks that the address is an OwnFlow vault. A wrong or unknown link offers no way to pay.
</Note>

## Where to see your income

- **Activity** shows each payment and how it was split.
- **My agents** shows what each agent earned. A call counts as earned when its transfer is seen on the blockchain.
