<Note>
  This page is a short outline for now. The full guide is being written.
</Note>

1. Log in.
2. **Choose your split.** The page starts with a sensible default. Move the sliders until the total is exactly 100%.
3. **Choose your stocks.** Search, tick the ones you want, and set a daily limit.
4. **Create my vault.** Signing this is also your approval of the stock list.
