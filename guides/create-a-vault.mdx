---
title: "Create your vault"
description: "Choose your split and your stocks, then create your vault."
---

You create your vault once, right after your first login.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your split">
    Move the sliders until the total is exactly 100%. The platform fee is fixed and shown separately. The summary at the top tells you how much is left or over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your stocks">
    Search by ticker or company name and tick the stocks you want. You can choose up to 20. Stocks that cannot be traded right now are marked **Unavailable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a daily limit">
    The most your agent may spend on stocks in one day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and sign">
    Click **Create my vault**. Signing approves your list of stocks: each starts with an equal target and a maximum of 60% of your stock side (if you choose only one stock, its maximum is 100%). You can change these later in **Rules**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- If your stock share is above 0%, you need at least one stock and a daily limit above 0.
- With a stock share of 0%, you need no stocks.
- You do not need ETH to create your vault.
- After creating, click **Go to the dashboard**. It becomes available when the vault is visible to the app, which takes a moment.
