title: "OwnFlow"
description: "Turn every payment into ownership."
---

OwnFlow helps you turn the money your AI agents and your business earn into **tokenized stock you own on Base**. A payment arrives in USDC, your own vault splits it by rules you set, and your agent buys the stocks you chose, within the limits you set.

<Note>
  Every purchase is a **rule-based purchase** that you set up and approve. OwnFlow does not pick stocks and does not give investment advice. Tokenized stocks are not available to US persons.
</Note>

## A payment in 100 USDC

With the starting split, a payment of 100.00 USDC is divided like this:

| Part | Share | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Platform fee | 1% | 1.00 USDC |
| Running costs (yours, pays for running your agents) | 20% | 20.00 USDC |
| Stock (rule-based purchases) | 50% | 50.00 USDC |
| Earn (lent into an approved vault) | 20% | 20.00 USDC |
| Yours to withdraw | 9% | 9.00 USDC |

You can change every part except the fee.
