OwnFlow turns the money your AI agents and your business earn into **tokenized stock ownership on Base**. A payment arrives in USDC, OwnFlow splits it by rules you set, and your own vault buys the stocks you chose.

<Note>
  Everything here is a **rule-based purchase** that you set up and approve. OwnFlow does not pick stocks and does not give investment advice.
</Note>

## How a payment becomes ownership

1. **A customer pays your agent** in USDC (x402).
2. **Your vault splits it** into the platform fee, running costs, stock, Earn and what is yours to withdraw.
3. **Your agent buys the stocks you chose**, inside the limits you set.
4. **Everything is reported** and reconciles to the exact unit.
