## What you need

- A login: email, Google or a wallet such as Base Account, Coinbase Wallet or MetaMask.
- A little USDC on Base to try a payment (on the test network you can get test USDC in the app).

## The steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in and create your vault">
    Choose how a payment is split and which stocks to include. Creating the vault is also your approval of that stock list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make an agent from a template">
    Pick a template, give it a name and a price, and test it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it and get paid">
    Share the agent's page. Every call is paid in USDC and lands in your vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the report">
    See each payment split, each purchase and each check that balances.
  </Step>
</Steps>
