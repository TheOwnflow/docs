## Before you start

- A login: **email**, **Google**, or a wallet such as Base Account, Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask or a WalletConnect wallet.
- **USDC on Base**. USDC on another network does not reach your vault.
- A little **ETH on Base**, only if you send USDC from your own wallet.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Click **Log in**. With email or Google, OwnFlow creates a wallet for you. That wallet is yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your split">
    The page starts with 20% running costs, 50% stock, 20% Earn and 9% yours to withdraw, plus the 1% fee. Move the sliders until the total is exactly 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your stocks">
    Tick up to 20 stocks and set a **daily limit**, the most your agent may spend on stocks in one day. Each stock starts with an equal target and a maximum of 60% of your stock side. A single stock has a maximum of 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your vault">
    Click **Create my vault** and sign. Signing also approves your list of stocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add money">
    Open **Funds** and choose **Add to vault**, or share your **Pay link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it work">
    **Overview** shows what you own. **Activity** shows every step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Small amounts wait. A split needs **1.00 USDC**, each stock purchase needs **0.50 USDC**, and Earn lends from **1.00 USDC**. With the starting split and two stocks, about 5.00 USDC of payments shows all three.
</Tip>

A wallet creates one vault, so keep access to it. With email or Google you can export your private key in **Settings**.

## Gas

**Gas** is the network fee of a blockchain action. With email, Google or an ordinary wallet such as MetaMask, OwnFlow pays it for the actions you sign. A smart wallet, such as Base Account, handles gas on its own side.

| Action | Do you need ETH? |
|---|---|
| Create your vault, change your rules | No |
| Withdraw, sell, pause, take everything out | No |
| Split a payment, add running costs from your wallet | No |
| Pay an agent | No, you only sign |
| Send USDC from your wallet to a vault | **Yes** |

If free gas is unavailable, your wallet asks you to pay it.
