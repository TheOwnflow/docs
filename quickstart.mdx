This guide takes you from nothing to a first rule-based purchase on the test network.

## Before you start

- A way to log in: **email**, **Google**, or a wallet such as Base Account, Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask or a WalletConnect wallet.
- Test USDC. The app links to the [Circle faucet](https://faucet.circle.com) for the Base test network.
- If you will send USDC **from your own wallet** (for example to your vault), a very small amount of test ETH on Base for gas. Creating a vault and changing your settings do not need it. See [Wallets and gas](/concepts/wallets-and-gas).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Click **Log in** and choose email, Google or your wallet. If you use email or Google, OwnFlow creates a wallet for you. That wallet is yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your split">
    The setup page starts with 5% running costs, 50% stock, 20% Earn and the rest for you to withdraw. Move the sliders until the total is exactly 100%. The page shows how much is **left** or how much you are **over**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your stocks">
    Search and tick the stocks you want. The page starts with a few chosen. Set a **daily limit**: the most your agent may spend on stocks in one day (it starts at 100 USDC).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your vault">
    Click **Create my vault** and sign. Signing is also your approval of that list of stocks. When it is ready, click **Go to the dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add some money">
    Open **Funds** and choose **Add to vault**, or share your **Pay link**. The payment is split by your rules, usually within a few minutes. The fee applies to money you add yourself too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch your first purchase">
    Within a few minutes your agent buys your stocks within your limits. Open **Overview** to see what you own, and **Activity** to see every step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Small payments wait. A split happens automatically once at least 1.00 USDC is waiting, and a purchase needs at least 1.00 USDC. You can also split at once with **Split it now** on your pay page.
</Tip>
